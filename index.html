    04 / Principles

    Shared by every app

    Built to be trusted.

    01

    Your files, your computer

    Notes are ordinary text files in a folder you own. Copy them, back them up, open them in anything. Nothing is locked in.

    02

    Look, don't touch

    ChatBridge only reads your AI chats. It never changes or deletes anything in your ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini account.

    03

    Nothing overwritten

    If a chat changes, the new version is saved next to the old one. Your history only ever grows.

    04

    Familiar from day one

    Layouts and shortcuts you already know from OneNote and the AI apps you use. Nothing new to learn.

    05

    No accounts, no tracking

    No sign-ups and no analytics. Brain Capture doesn't even ask for internet access: your voice never leaves the phone.

    06

    Free & open

    ChatBridge and OneNote Style are free and open source. Anyone can read exactly what they do on GitHub.

    05 / Get started

    Never used Obsidian? Perfect.

    Up and running in ten minutes.

    1. 1

      Get Obsidian

      It's a free notes app. Install it, then choose Create new vault. A vault is just a folder where your notes will live.

      obsidian.md/download
    2. 2

      Pick an app

      Open any app above and follow its steps. Each one has an easy way (a couple of clicks) and a by-hand way.

      Choose an app
    3. 3

      Switch it on

      In Obsidian go to Settings → Community plugins, turn it on, and you're done. Restart Obsidian if it doesn't show up straight away.

    Questions friends ask

    What's a "second brain"?

    A single place where everything you'd like to remember ends up: notes, ideas, voice memos, saved articles and the answers AI gave you. Instead of hunting through five apps, you search one.

    Do I need to be technical?

    No. If you can install an app and click through a settings page, you can do this. Each app has step-by-step instructions written for normal people.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Obsidian is free for personal use, and these apps are free.

    Does anything get uploaded?

    No. Your notes stay in a folder on your computer. ChatBridge downloads your own chats to that folder; Brain Capture works without internet at all. There's no server of mine in between.

    Is this made by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or Microsoft?

    No. These are independent, unofficial tools. They're not affiliated with or endorsed by any of those companies.

    Could it mess up my ChatGPT or Claude account?

    ChatBridge only reads, the same way your browser does when you open a chat. It never sends, edits or deletes anything. Because it's unofficial, a website change can occasionally break syncing until an update comes out.

    Does it work on my phone or Mac?

    ChatBridge and OneNote Style run in Obsidian on Windows, macOS and Linux computers. Brain Capture is an Android app and is still in private testing.
